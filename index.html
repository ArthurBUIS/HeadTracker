Merge zone (X:9): streams merge when a head enters the inner X:9 core of another; split when it leaves the full 16:9 box
Box overlap: % of the smaller box that must overlap to merge / stay merged (keep merge ≥ split)
Lost duration: how many missed detections a lost stream is kept (greyed) before it drops
Disengagement: a detected head with no face for this many rounds is greyed as “disengaged” (loads a MobileNet face model)
Score threshold: detections below this confidence are ignored
Video speed: slow the source video down (applies to loaded files)
